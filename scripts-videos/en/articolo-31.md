## Escena 3

**Imagen:** Lista de documentos

**Voz:**

> Usually needed: the relative's passport, the child's birth certificate and proof of the relationship, proof that the child lives in Italy (residence or hospitality), school or paediatrician certificates, and anything showing why separation would harm the child.
> Cost: no contributo unificato, but many courts ask for a fixed advance of about €27 (revenue stamp or pagoPA). Check with your court.
